Risk andresponsibility.

An investment in Babylon Capital AB involves risk. The value of an investment may rise or fall. There is no guarantee that the investor will recover the full amount of the invested capital.

Read before making an investment decision.This information is a summary and does not replace complete company information, agreements or professional legal, tax and financial advice.
Key risks

The risks behind the investment.

The value of Babylon Capital AB is affected by the company’s investments and the performance of the underlying asset management. These risks should be considered from a long-term perspective and in relation to the investor’s own financial circumstances.

Market risk

Prices in commodities, currencies, interest rates and equity indices may change rapidly. Economic, political and geopolitical events may therefore affect the company’s value both positively and negatively.

Trend-following performance profile

Trend-following strategies may experience repeated smaller losses in sideways and volatile markets. Periods of weak performance and drawdowns are a natural feature of this form of asset management.
