About the company

Babylon Capital AB

Babylon Capital AB was established in its current form in 2026 as a Swedish holding company for long-term asset management. The company brings investors together in a shared structure in which the capital is managed for the company’s own account.

In the first capital raise, shares were subscribed at a price of SEK 10. The company’s development is reported regularly through its share price, investor letters and investor meetings.

Henrik Hallenborg

Henrik Hallenborg is responsible for Babylon Capital AB’s asset management and capital allocation and has invested his own capital in the company. He also works as a Commodity Trading Advisor through Hallenborg CTA, focusing on systematic and trend-following trading.

Hallenborg CTA registered as a Commodity Trading Advisor in 2012. The business is a member of the National Futures Association and is subject to the Commodity Futures Trading Commission’s regulatory framework.

With roots in the financial markets since the mid-1990s and active trading since 1997, his experience has been shaped by several market cycles – from the dot-com bubble and the financial crisis to the zero-interest-rate environment and today’s inflation-driven conditions.

Henrik Hallenborg is a co-founder of Börstjänaren and has more than two decades of experience in asset management and market analysis. During the commodity boom of 2007–2008, he produced ongoing analysis and market letters for the Royal Bank of Scotland, focusing on global macro trends.

Hallenborg is Swedish and lives in Spain, with international experience including the United States, where he previously worked in Dallas, Texas.

His investment philosophy is trend following and is influenced by the Austrian School of Economics, with an emphasis on market processes, incentives and discipline in capital allocation.

In the preface to the book Managed Futures – Follow the crowd from a distance, Henrik writes more about his background and his path to systematic trading.

CEO · Babylon Capital AB

Fredrik Swahn

Fredrik Swahn is an entrepreneur and organisation builder with a background in technology, economics and sport. Since the early 2000s, he has led businesses and projects at the intersection of performance, health and business development, with a particular focus on ambassadorship, long-term thinking and operational execution.

As the founder of Mantra Sport, he has developed activities in events, sales, travel, physiological testing and sport since 2002.

Swahn worked with Börstjänaren for eight years, contributing to the development and operational work of the business.

In addition to his own business activities, he has assisted entrepreneurs during the start-up phase – from business concept to establishment – in areas including business structure, marketing, project management and administration. His experience covers both practical execution and strategic planning in small and medium-sized organisations.

Swahn holds a Master of Science in Engineering Physics from Uppsala University, with additional studies in entrepreneurship, economics and law. His interdisciplinary background combines an analytical foundation with commercial understanding.

As an athlete, he has competed at national level, winning several Swedish championship medals in duathlon and achieving notable results in running, including Swedish masters records in recent years.

Fredrik Swahn is CEO of Babylon Capital AB and has invested his own capital in the company. As CEO, he is responsible for the company’s operations, structure and administration, with a focus on building a robust platform for asset management.

His work is characterised by discipline, transparency and a long-term perspective on value creation.
