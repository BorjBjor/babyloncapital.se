Privacy policy

This privacy policy describes how Babylon Capital AB processes personal data when you visit the website, contact us, register for an investor event or subscribe to investor letters.

1. Data controller

Babylon Capital AB is the data controller for the processing described in this policy. Questions about personal data and requests to exercise your rights can be submitted through our contact page.

2. Personal data we process

We may process your name, email address, telephone number, information contained in messages, event registration details and other information you provide. The website may also process limited technical data required for functionality, security and troubleshooting.

3. Purposes and legal basis

We process personal data to respond to enquiries and take action at your request, administer investor events, send investor letters and other information you have requested, comply with legal obligations, and protect the website and our legitimate interests.

Depending on the circumstances, processing is based on consent, steps taken before entering into or performing a contract, a legal obligation, or a balancing of legitimate interests.

4. Investor letters and consent

When you subscribe to investor letters, we process your name and email address based on your consent. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

5. Investor events and contact forms

Information submitted during registration is used to administer the relevant event and communicate with you about your registration. Information submitted through contact forms is used to respond to and follow up your enquiry.

6. Retention

Contact details and messages are retained for as long as necessary to handle and follow up the matter. Information about an investor event is deleted once the event and necessary administrative follow-up have been completed. Information used for investor letters is retained until you withdraw your consent or the service ends. Data may be retained for longer when required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

7. Recipients and service providers

We may engage service providers for web hosting, form processing, technical operations and email distribution. These providers may process personal data only in accordance with our instructions and applicable data protection legislation.

8. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

If a service provider processes personal data outside the EU/EEA, we ensure that the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision, the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or another applicable safeguard.

9. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data. You may also request restriction, object to processing based on legitimate interests and, in certain cases, receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection. We do not use personal data for decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects for you.

10. Cookies and external services

The website uses Google Analytics to compile statistics on matters such as pages visited, traffic sources, device type and downloads. The analytics tool is activated only if you choose to accept analytics cookies. If you reject them, Google Analytics is not loaded.

Your choice is stored locally in your browser and can be withdrawn by deleting the website’s stored data and making a new choice on your next visit. Data processed through Google Analytics may include technical information about your browser and device, approximate geographical information, visit data and interactions with the website. Google processes data under its own terms and data protection rules.

Technically necessary storage may also be used for website functionality, security and protection against misuse. Links to external websites are governed by the privacy policy of the respective website.

11. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration and unlawful processing.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when our processing activities or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version is published on this page.
