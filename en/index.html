Method

Systematic management. Clear rules.

Babylon Capital invests in trend-following strategies that follow price movements across global financial markets. The approach is based on a systematic method, broad diversification and disciplined risk-taking.

Trading includes commodities, currencies, interest rates and equity indices and is governed by rule-based models with clearly defined risk parameters.

Why Managed Futures?

A different approach.

  1. 01 Follows trends – upwards and downwards
  2. 02 Trades in global futures markets
  3. 03 Based on price movements, not economic forecasts
  4. 04 Can take both long and short positions
  5. 05 Adds a different type of market exposure from traditional assets
A consistent method

Trend before
opinion.

No one can know the next major market move with certainty. We therefore focus on what can be controlled: method, diversification and discipline.

01

Identify the move

Systematic models look for established trends across many different markets.

02

Manage risk

Position sizes are adjusted to volatility. No single trade is intended to determine the overall outcome.

03

Let the trend work

Loss-making positions are reduced according to the rules, while winning positions are given room to develop.
