Return profile

Volatility is part of trend following.

Negative months exceeding 10 per cent can occur when previously unrealised gains are given back as markets consolidate or change direction.

This is a natural part of trend-following trading and is often necessary in order to retain positions through longer and stronger trends. During powerful trend phases, volatility in open positions can increase, creating larger swings in monthly returns before the trend ends.
