About the book

A complement to equities and bonds.

Managed Futures is an investment approach that can take both long and short positions in global futures markets.

In the book, Henrik Hallenborg explains how Managed Futures developed, why trends exist and how CTA managers work with models, diversification and risk management.

The reader follows the development from commodity trading and open-outcry floors to electronic, rules-based and AI-driven markets. The book also examines Crisis Alpha, manager risk and how trend following has historically been analysed during periods when traditional portfolios were under pressure. Trend following does not guarantee positive returns or protection against loss during market declines.

Following the crowd from a distance means respecting market movements – without being consumed by them.
